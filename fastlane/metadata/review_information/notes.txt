2. WHY THIS APP USES BLUETOOTH BACKGROUND MODES
The one thing worth reading. Two people work a match: one tracks serves, the other coaches. Each Apple Watch pairs to its own phone and to nothing else, so the court cannot reach the coach's wrist from the tracker's phone directly. It goes phone to phone over Bluetooth LE, and her phone feeds her own watch.

bluetooth-peripheral is declared because the tracking phone advertises the match. bluetooth-central is declared because the coach's phone subscribes to it. Both are needed: with only one, half the pair stops working.

They are background modes because a coach puts her phone in her pocket. Without them iOS suspends the app and the link drops -- exactly what happened in testing: the match stopped arriving whenever her screen locked, and her watch and lock screen silently stopped moving. Nothing travels but the serves of the match in progress, between two devices in one gym, and only while sharing has been switched on by hand.

4. WHAT THE APP DOES AND FOR WHOM
Volleyball Serve Tracker records which player served and whether each serve went in, out, or won the point. It replaces the paper serve chart a parent or assistant coach keeps on the sideline at a youth volleyball match, totalling serve-in percentage and points per player continuously so the coach has figures in front of them when deciding on a substitution. Audience: parents, assistant coaches and managers of school and club teams.

5. HOW TO REACH EVERY FEATURE
No credentials or sample files are needed. From a fresh install:
- Roster tab: type a name and jersey number, tap Add. Repeat six times.
- Track tab: tap Start game. Tap an empty court box, then a player, six times.
- Tap the player in the bottom-right box to hand them the ball, then record serves with OUT / IN / IN-POINT. Rotation advances itself; Undo reverses one action.
- After five serves by one player a full-screen alert appears; any tap clears it.
- The two numbers at the top are the rally score. They take a tap only while your side is not serving: a serve's own outcome already decides that rally.
- "Share this match" starts sharing. A second phone running the app sees it offered and taps once to watch.
- Game tab shows running figures. Season tab holds past games, notes, backup, restore and corrections.
- Apple Watch: mirrors the phone's court. Swipe up twice for the standalone scoreboard, which needs no phone.

6. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. No data providers, authentication, payments, AI, analytics, advertising SDK, crash reporter or social login, and no internet code of any kind -- a test fails the build if any shipped file references an internet networking API. All data stays on the user's own devices, hence the "Data Not Collected" privacy answer. The only outbound link is an optional "buy me a coffee" tip that opens in Safari; the app takes no payments.
