No account or login is needed.

To try the phone app: Roster tab, add six or more players with numbers. Track tab, Start game. Tap an empty spot on the court and then a player to place them, six times, or tap a player and then a spot — both work. Tap the player in the bottom-right corner to hand them the ball, then record with OUT / IN / IN — POINT.

The Apple Watch app mirrors the phone's court over Watch Connectivity and needs a paired watch with the phone app open. Its scoreboard page — swipe up twice — works on its own with no phone at all, if pairing is not convenient.

The app contains no networking code of any kind. Nothing recorded leaves the device, which is why the privacy answer is "Data Not Collected".
