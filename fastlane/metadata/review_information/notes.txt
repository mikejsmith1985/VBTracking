2. DEVICES AND OS TESTED
Physical hardware: iPhone 17 Pro (iOS 26.6) and Apple Watch Ultra 2 (watchOS 26.6, 23U67). Builds were distributed through TestFlight and used to record real matches before submission. Minimum supported: iOS 18.0, watchOS 11.0. An automated interface suite also drives a full match on the iPhone simulator on every build, and a layout suite checks the watch court at all seven Apple Watch sizes down to 40 mm.

3. WHAT THE APP DOES AND FOR WHOM
Volleyball Serve Tracker records which player served and whether each serve went in, out, or won the point. It replaces the paper serve chart a parent or assistant coach keeps on the sideline at a youth volleyball match, and totals serve-in percentage and points per player continuously so the coach has figures in front of them when deciding on a substitution. The Apple Watch app mirrors the court onto the coach's wrist. Audience: parents, assistant coaches and team managers of school and club volleyball teams.

4. HOW TO SET UP AND REACH EVERY FEATURE
No credentials or sample files are needed. From a fresh install:
- Roster tab: type a name and jersey number, tap Add. Repeat six or more times.
- Track tab: tap Start game. Tap an empty court box, then a player, six times.
- Tap the player in the bottom-right box to hand them the ball, then record serves with OUT / IN / IN-POINT. Rotation advances by itself; Undo reverses one action.
- After five serves by one player a full-screen alert appears; any tap clears it.
- Game tab shows running figures. Season tab holds past games, notes, backup and restore, and per-serve corrections.
- Apple Watch: mirrors the phone's court over Watch Connectivity while the phone app is open. Swipe up twice on the watch for the standalone scoreboard page, which needs no phone.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
None. No data providers, authentication, payments, AI, analytics, advertising SDK, crash reporter or social login. The app contains no networking code; an automated test fails the build if any shipped source file references a networking API. All data stays on the device, hence the "Data Not Collected" privacy answer. The only outbound link is an optional "buy me a coffee" tip link that opens in Safari; the app takes no payments.

The app functions identically in every region, ships in English only, is not in a regulated industry, and contains no third-party material. All artwork and text are the developer's own.
