2. WHY THIS APP USES BLUETOOTH BACKGROUND MODES
This is the one thing worth reading. Two people work a match: one tracks the serves, the other coaches. Their Apple Watches pair to their own phones and to nothing else, so the court cannot travel from the tracker's phone to the coach's wrist directly. It goes phone to phone over Bluetooth LE, and the coach's phone feeds her own watch.

bluetooth-peripheral is declared because the tracking phone advertises the match. bluetooth-central is declared because the coach's phone subscribes to it. Both are needed: with only one, half the pair stops working.

They are background modes because a coach puts her phone in her pocket. Without them iOS suspends the app and the link drops, which is exactly what happened in testing: the match stopped arriving every time her screen locked, and the court on her watch and lock screen silently stopped moving. Nothing is transmitted but the serves of the match in progress, between two devices in the same gym, and only while the operator has switched sharing on by hand.

4. WHAT THE APP DOES AND FOR WHOM
Volleyball Serve Tracker records which player served and whether each serve went in, out, or won the point. It replaces the paper serve chart a parent or assistant coach keeps on the sideline at a youth volleyball match, and totals serve-in percentage and points per player continuously so the coach has figures in front of them when deciding on a substitution. Audience: parents, assistant coaches and team managers of school and club volleyball teams.

5. HOW TO REACH EVERY FEATURE
No credentials or sample files are needed. From a fresh install:
- Roster tab: type a name and jersey number, tap Add. Repeat six or more times.
- Track tab: tap Start game. Tap an empty court box, then a player, six times.
- Tap the player in the bottom-right box to hand them the ball, then record serves with OUT / IN / IN-POINT. Rotation advances by itself; Undo reverses one action.
- After five serves by one player a full-screen alert appears; any tap clears it.
- The two numbers at the top are the rally score. They take a tap only while your side is not serving, because a serve's own outcome already decides that rally.
- "Share this match" starts sharing. A second phone running the app sees it offered and taps once to watch.
- Game tab shows running figures. Season tab holds past games, notes, backup, restore and per-serve corrections.
- Apple Watch: mirrors the phone's court. Swipe up twice for the standalone scoreboard, which needs no phone.

6. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. No data providers, authentication, payments, AI, analytics, advertising SDK, crash reporter or social login, and no internet code of any kind -- an automated test fails the build if any shipped source file references an internet networking API. All data stays on the user's own devices, hence the "Data Not Collected" privacy answer. The only outbound link is an optional "buy me a coffee" tip link that opens in Safari; the app takes no payments.
