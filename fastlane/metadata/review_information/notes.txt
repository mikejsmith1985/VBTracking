WHAT THE APP DOES

Volleyball Serve Tracker records which player served, and whether each serve
went in, out, or won the point. It is a replacement for the paper clipboard a
parent or assistant coach keeps on the sideline at a youth volleyball match.
From those serves it derives each player's serve-in percentage and points, per
game, per match and across a season.

Target audience: parents, assistant coaches and team managers of school and
club volleyball teams, mostly youth level.

The problem it solves: a serve chart kept on paper cannot be totalled during a
match, so the coach making a substitution decision has no figures in front of
them. The app totals continuously, and the Apple Watch app puts the court on the
coach's wrist so they can see who serves next without holding a phone.

HOW TO SET UP AND REACH EVERY FEATURE

No credentials and no sample files are needed. From a fresh install:

1. Roster tab. Type a name and a jersey number, tap Add. Do this six or more
   times. (Six is a volleyball rotation.)
2. Track tab. Tap "Start game".
3. Build the rotation: tap an empty box on the court, then tap a player to put
   them there. Repeat six times. Tapping the player first and then the box works
   equally well.
4. Tap the player in the bottom-right box to hand them the ball.
5. Record serves with the three buttons: OUT, IN, IN - POINT. The rotation
   advances by itself when a serve is lost. Undo reverses one action at a time.
6. After five serves by one player a full-screen alert appears; any tap clears
   it and a sixth serve is still recorded normally.
7. Game tab shows the running figures. Season tab holds past games, notes, and
   "Save a copy of everything" / "Restore from a saved copy".
8. Any past serve can be corrected: Season tab, pick a game, open a serve turn.

APPLE WATCH APP

It mirrors the phone's court over Watch Connectivity and needs a paired watch
with the phone app open. Swipe up twice on the watch to reach the scoreboard
page, which works entirely on its own with no phone involved if pairing is not
convenient during review.

EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS

None. There are no data providers, no authentication service, no payment
processor, no AI service, no analytics, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter
and no social login. The app contains no networking code of any kind -- an
automated test fails the build if any shipped source file so much as names a
networking API. Nothing recorded leaves the device, which is why the privacy
answer is "Data Not Collected".

The only link out of the app is an optional "buy me a coffee" tip link, which
opens in Safari. The app takes no payments and never learns whether it was
followed.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
