Privacy Policy

Volleyball Serve Tracker · Root Level Labs · Last updated 30 August 2026

This app collects nothing. It has no analytics, no accounts, no adverts, no trackers, and no networking code of any kind. Nothing you record leaves your device unless you choose to export it and send it somewhere yourself.

What is stored, and where

Everything you record — seasons, players, jersey numbers, games, and every serve — is written to storage on your own iPhone. The Apple Watch app holds a copy of the current court and any serves it has recorded but not yet handed to the phone.

Neither copy is sent to us, and neither is sent to anyone else. We operate no server for this app and hold no database of its users. We cannot see your data, because there is nowhere for it to arrive.

The link between your phone and your watch

The two apps talk over Apple's Watch Connectivity, which is a direct link between two devices you have paired to each other. It needs no internet connection, no router, and no account. Nothing about that link involves us or any third party.

Backups you make yourself

The app can save a copy of everything as a single file. Where that file goes, and who you give it to, is entirely your decision. Apple's own iCloud and device backups may include the app's data if you have those switched on, under Apple's terms rather than ours.

Children

The app is designed for a coach or a parent tracking a team, and it may well hold the names and jersey numbers of young players. Those stay on your phone, exactly like the rest of your data. We do not collect them, and we have no way to.

Third parties

There are none. The app bundles no analytics kit, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter, and no social login. The only link out of the app is an optional one to Buy Me a Coffee, and it opens in your browser — the app does not take payments, ask for card details, or know whether you followed it.

Your rights

Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to show you, correct, or delete. Deleting the app from your phone deletes everything it recorded. Export a copy first if you want to keep it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a future version of the app ever collects anything at all, that will be said here plainly and before it ships.
