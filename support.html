Common questions

Where is my data?
On your phone, and nowhere else. The app has no accounts and no server. See the privacy policy.
How do I move a season to a new phone?
Season → Your data → Save a copy of everything. That gives you one file. On the new phone, Season → Your data → Restore from a saved copy. Do this before you delete the app from the old phone: deleting it deletes the record.
Why does a figure show a dash instead of a zero?
Because it was never recorded. A game entered from a paper sheet holds serves but no points, and reporting those points as nought would say the players earned none. A dash means nobody wrote it down.
I entered a serve wrong. Can I fix it?
Yes, including in games from weeks ago. Season → pick the game → open a serve turn. You can change a serve's outcome, add or remove one, give the turn to a different player, delete the turn, or add a turn that was missed at the time.
The watch says a serve was not sent.
It is safe. The watch keeps every serve until the phone confirms it, and delivery survives going out of range. Open the app on the phone with the watch nearby and the message clears.
Can I stop the watch buzzing at five serves?
Yes. Swipe to the last page on the watch and choose Off, Brief or Persistent.
Does it work with no signal?
Yes. It has no networking in it at all, which is checked on every build. Gyms rarely have usable signal, so nothing in the app waits on one. The watch link is Bluetooth between your own two devices, so aeroplane mode will stop that — but nothing else.
Does it cost anything?
No. No adverts, nothing locked, no account. There is an optional tip jar if it saved you a clipboard.
